# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 6, 2026

WinOTP ("we", "our", or "us") operates the WinOTP application (the "Service"). This page explains our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our Service.

## Information We Collect

WinOTP is designed to protect your privacy. We do **not** collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.

- No account registration is required.
- No personal information is collected.
- No analytics or tracking tools are used.

## Local Data Storage

All OTP secrets and authentication data are stored locally on your device only.

- Data never leaves your device.
- No cloud synchronization is performed by WinOTP.
- No external servers are involved for storing OTP secrets.

## Permissions

The application may request the following permission:

- **Camera Access**: Used only when you choose to scan a QR code to add an OTP account.

Camera access is optional and is used only for QR code scanning.

## Data Security

We take your privacy seriously. Sensitive OTP data is stored on your device and is not transmitted over the internet by WinOTP.

## Third-Party Services

WinOTP does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services that collect user data.

## Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
